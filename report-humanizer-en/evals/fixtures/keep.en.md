## Trial background

This report includes 4 Phase 3 trials. Inclusion criteria were randomized design, at least 300 patients, and a primary endpoint of PFS or OS; single-arm studies and exploratory cohorts were excluded.

Endpoints and assessment time points differ across the trials, so the reported values are not directly comparable. This subgroup analysis was post-hoc and does not support a conclusion on the primary endpoint.

Data cutoff was 2025-06-30. The source did not report OS for this subgroup, so no estimate is given. The mechanism of the hepatic signal remains unclear, reflecting the limits of the available laboratory data. In terms of severity, the reported events were mostly grade 1-2: 61 patients (18%) had grade >=3 events, and 12 of these had no assessment of causality.

KRYSTAL-12 planned to enroll 560 patients and had not yet started as of the cutoff. Median follow-up was 18.4 months.
