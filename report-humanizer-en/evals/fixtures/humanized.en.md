## Trial background

KRYSTAL-12 is a Phase 3 study that enrolled 847 patients with KRAS G12C-mutant NSCLC; the primary endpoint was progression-free survival (PFS). Median PFS was 9.4 months versus 6.1 months in the control arm (HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.48-0.80, p<0.001). Grade >=3 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 18% versus 12%, and the discontinuation rate was 4%.

As of the data cutoff 2025-06-30, the OS analysis was not mature; median follow-up was 18.4 months. KRYSTAL-12 planned to enroll 560 patients and had not yet started. The source did not report the OS subgroup.
