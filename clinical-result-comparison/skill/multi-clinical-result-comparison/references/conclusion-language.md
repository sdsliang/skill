# Conclusion Language

## Evidence-calibrated wording

Use language that separates observation from proof.

### Strong within-source or head-to-head support

- “原文报告 A 相比 B 改善……”
- “该随机对照结果支持……”
- “在该试验、该人群和该时间点内，A 优于 B……”

### Indirect cross-trial signal

- “各研究试验组在对应人群和时间点的观察结果如下；该并列不构成头对头比较……”
- “在已对齐的维度上，结果 A 呈现更有利的方向……”
- “结果 A 的数值更高/更低，但跨试验差异不能证明治疗优效……”
- “研究内对照支持该试验自身的效应判断，但不能转换成对另一研究试验组的直接比较……”
- “研究设计和人群差异使该观察只能作为方向性参考……”

### Insufficient evidence

- “现有原文不足以判断……”
- “两项结果回答的临床问题不同，不形成有效的优劣比较……”
- “安全性定义或暴露时间不一致，不能据此判定谁更安全……”

### Same-trial evidence chain

- “后续分析新增了……”
- “该结果更新了更早的估计……”
- “两份来源使用相同数据截止点，应视为重复披露而非独立验证……”
- “证据从……发展为……，因此整个试验的判断得到加强/基本不变/受到限定/削弱/仍无法判断……”

## Statistical and clinical meaning

Only call a result statistically significant if the text reports adequate statistical support. “Nominally significant” remains nominal and must not be upgraded. Distinguish statistical support from clinical relevance; if clinical importance thresholds are absent, do not invent them.

## Safety wording

- “未报告” means unknown in the supplied source, not zero events.
- “未发现新信号” does not mean no risk.
- “可管理” is a source interpretation unless supported by event severity, discontinuation, management, and exposure data.
- Different follow-up or exposure prevents simple percentage ranking.

## Winner wording

Prefer a matrix of dimension-specific conclusions. Use “directionally favored” only for directly or partially aligned endpoints; context-only or unaligned results receive no comparative direction. Use “综合更优” only when the cross-trial workflow's winner conditions are met. Otherwise say:

- “没有可辩护的综合胜者”；
- “结果 A 在 X 维度方向占优，但整体优劣无法确定”； or
- “这些结果属于不同临床问题，应分别解读”。

Never compare “how positive” results sound across different clinical questions. A positive body-composition endpoint, a negative symptom endpoint, and a long-term safety analysis do not form one efficacy ranking.

对于同一试验的多份来源，不评价哪一份披露胜出。应说明整个试验的证据链是加强、基本不变、受到限定、削弱还是仍无法判断，并指出造成变化的具体分析状态。
